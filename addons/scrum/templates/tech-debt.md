# Technical Debt

Record deliberate shortcuts, known deviations from coding standards, and deferred refactors. Sprint planning reads this log for remediation candidates. Do not delete resolved entries; mark them resolved with date and reference.

## Entry Format

```markdown
### TD-NNN: {Short title}

**Area**: {Backend | Frontend | Infrastructure | ...}
**Files**: `{path}`
**Issue**: {What deviates and why it matters.}
**Fix**: {What needs to change.}
**Impact**: {Current consequence and who is affected.}
**Status**: Open | Resolved {YYYY-MM-DD, reference}
```

Priority is expressed by the section an entry lives in.
